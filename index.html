Why we are called Koino
κοινωνία

koinonia · koy-no-NEE-ah · Greek for fellowship, communion, deep intimacy

It’s the word used for the early church sharing real life together (Acts 2:42). Koino exists for that same thing: to move people from being near each other to truly knowing each other.

Questions

Before you start.

How do I get into the app?

After checkout, download the free Whop app (iPhone or Android), sign in with the email you paid with, and open Koino. Most people are playing within two minutes. It also works on a computer at whop.com.

Do we each need a phone?

No. One phone is enough. Pass it around, or read the card out loud. Koino is made for being together, not staring at separate screens.

Is Koino only for couples?

Not at all. There are decks for first dates, friends, whole families, parents and children, kids, siblings and people reflecting alone. You choose who you’re with and Koino shows the right decks.

Is it Christian?

Koino is faith-rooted. There are faith decks like Questions Jesus Asked and Psalms of the Heart, but most decks welcome everyone, whatever they believe.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. Every card is clean. Kids decks use simple, joyful words, and nothing in Koino is crude or explicit.

Can’t I just find free questions online?

You can find lists. Koino gives you 2,700+ cards written to feel human, not like an interview, organised by who you’re with, with games, a daily card, reminders and a streak that keep you coming back.

What if it’s not for us?

Then you shouldn’t pay for it. Message us on Whop within 7 days of your purchase and we’ll refund you in full. No questions asked.

How do I cancel?

From your Whop account, in a couple of taps. No calls, no emails.
